# Clip pipelines

All three builders end in the same place: a recipe JSON → `studio clip render` → automatic checks → contact sheets + a
transcript of the final audio, reviewed before anything is scheduled.

## 1. Game-demo clips: `fgclip.py` (ForgeGUI campaign)

**Input footage:** silent screen recordings of an AI tool building Roblox games from typed prompts.

1. **Read the footage.** `prompts.py` samples frames at 4 fps, runs Apple Vision OCR (a small Swift CLI) on the prompt box, and groups the text into prompts with start and end times. A contact sheet shows the payoffs: explosions, counters, chases.
2. **Write a spec** (`specs/fg004r_gojo.py` etc.):
   - `cuts`: `(name, src_in, src_out, speed)`. Speed `0` makes a freeze-frame beat mid-edit, e.g. holding on the city while "would you lose?" lands.
   - `lines`: narrator lines placed at `(cut, source time)`, so each prompt is read the moment it's typed.
   - `sfx`, `typing` ticks, `fx`: meme GIF cut-ins, impact frames, shake.
   - `titles`: a hook, plus stamps on payoffs.
   - `captions` with highlight words, and a logo window.
3. **Voice.** Every line is generated by the channel's cloned narrator, re-transcribed, kept only if it matches word for word, then placed on the edit timeline. Overlaps are warned about.
4. **Render + check.** A music bed is ducked under the voice. Brand-word check: "forge gui" must be heard.

What changed after feedback: plain prompt reading → **winning format**. That meant a rant hook ("Roblox is officially cooked…"), memes that fit the moment (the "Nah, I'd win" Gojo panel for an overconfident line, Jack Black's "CHICKEN JOCKEY!" for a chicken chase, the 67 kid when the narrator says "six seven million hay"), impact frames on hits, and coloured word captions.

## 2. Voice-led stories: `story.py` (Animal Valley campaign)

**Constraint:** only the brand's footage is allowed, so no outside memes. The brief also requires beat-synced cuts.

1. The narrator reads short lines (e.g. "Day one. One bear. One tree. Zero acorns.").
2. Each shot starts on a cue (a line, or a word inside a line) and is **snapped to the nearest beat** of the music (librosa beat tracking; the track is started so a beat lands on the first cut).
3. Stamps ("DAY 30", "2.51 TRILLION 🌰"), one fitting sound per beat, the logo from the line that says the game's name.
4. `flow.check_windows` warns if a source window contains an in-game camera snap, so shots are moved to clean stretches.
5. Every number said on screen is checked against what the footage actually shows (no false claims).

Formats made: "rating my employees", "every group project has these 3 animals", "downloaded a cozy game to fall asleep" (clock stamps
11:02 PM → 6:00 AM ⏰), a nature-documentary parody with a designed narrator voice, and "day 1 vs day 30".

## 3. Podcast clips: `podclip.py` (Mark Manson campaign)

1. Download the official episodes the brief points to, with the **original English audio** (they carry auto-dubbed tracks), and transcribe them.
2. **Pick moments** (a subagent plus a scoring rubric): one complete idea, 20–60 s, starting and ending on sentence boundaries, the host speaking first (the brief requires his face in the first 5 s), no ad reads, nothing that changes his meaning.
3. **Face-tracked crop.** Sample the range at 4 fps and detect camera cuts (frame difference) and the largest face per frame (YuNet). Each camera shot becomes a segment with a steady crop on the face. Wide shots zoom in (up to 1.6×) with headroom.
4. **Pacing.** Long shots are split at word gaps about every 3.5 s, alternating wide and a 1.14× punch-in.
5. **Text.** Captions come from the transcript (swears censored on screen, audio untouched), with a hook headline for the first 3.5 s.
6. **Audio.** Original only. Music is optional and very quiet, per the research.

## Shared tools

| Tool | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `verify.py` | Transcript of what's actually heard + integrated loudness + length, for any render |
| `make_kit.py` / `schedule.py` | Per-post folder (YouTube file + Instagram 8 Mbps copy + `POST THIS.txt`) and the Studio Schedule page, from one source of text |
| `memes.py` | Tenor search → numbered contact sheet → pick → download into the local meme library |
| `reframe.py` | Face tracking for horizontal → vertical crops |
| `checks.py` | The automatic QA list (see architecture) |
